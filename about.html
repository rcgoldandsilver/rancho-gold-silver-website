About Us

Family-owned. Three generations of precious-metals experience.

A Straightforward Way to Buy & Sell Precious Metals

Our family brings three generations of precious-metals knowledge to Rancho Cucamonga and San Bernardino. We focus on efficient local offices, professional testing and clear evaluations without commissioned salespeople pushing customers.

Items are tested and weighed in front of you whenever practical. For appropriate larger lots, our in-house precious metal assay and melting process can help create a consistent sample before an offer.
