Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome at both locations.

Are appraisals free?

Yes. Appraisals are free and there is no obligation to sell.

How do you test gold and silver?

We use professional methods including XRF, Sigma verification and traditional testing when appropriate.

Do you buy broken jewelry, silverware, and dental gold?

Yes. We buy many forms of precious metals, including broken items and scrap.

How do I get paid when I sell?

When you accept our offer, payment is immediate. Available methods include cash, business check, Zelle and wire transfer.
